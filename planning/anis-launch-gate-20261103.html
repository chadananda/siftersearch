Anís Launch Gate, 3 November

Four batteries were run against production on 3 Nov, 17:30–18:15 UTC: lookups, short turns, the W3 strategies and the W8 audience questions. Each number below names the file it came from. Anything not measured is marked as not measured.

Verdict: the gate does not pass today. Telling new questions from follow-ups passes (98.5%). Every bar on lookups and quality fails, and strategy routing misses narrowly (88.9% against 90%).

The biggest finding is the gap between channels. On the same questions, email beats Perplexity on 24/27 W3 questions and 18/18 answered W8 questions. Site chat does so on only 12/27 and 3/42. Every earlier battery went through the email path, so this gap had never been measured.

7Open decisions for Chad

  1. The welcome letter's corpus numbers are unverified. Either run scripts/corpus-status.mjs against production and use the measured figures, or take the numbers out.
  2. Strict route accuracy is below 90% (88.9% after W2; W3 strategy choice is also 24/27 = 88.9% today). Either launch at 88.9% or hold for ≥90%. Two of the three W3 misses are timeouts (defect 5), not wrong choices.
  3. The dossiers. The W8 doc recommends pulling both forward as data fixes. For Gate of the Heart: headings, and re-ingest without the ~665 junk paragraphs. For The Dawn-Breakers: year, slug, the three stub records and the [pg N] markers.
  4. New from today's data: which engine answers site chat? Email (agent) wins 24/27 and 18/18 at a p50 of 26–28 s and about $0.13 a turn (measured). Chat (pipeline) answers in 6 s for about $0.003 but wins 12/27 and 3/42. The choice is to put site-chat in ANIS_AGENT with a progress stream, or to bring the pipeline up to the bar before 9 Nov.

8Method, cost and caveats

  • Everything ran against production: /api/chat/stream, /api/v1/anis/draft and the admin thread view. There was no database access.
  • Spend. Measured: Anís server cost for battery@ turns since 17:50 UTC is $7.75 (61 turns); the chat-stream runs cost $0.27 (lookups $0.05, W3 $0.09, W8 $0.13). Estimated: about 185 Opus judge calls at roughly $0.05, so about $9, plus about 140 Perplexity calls, about $0.8. Total about $17, over the $15 cap. Two causes: the leftover runs, and the second judging pass needed to measure site chat.
  • The discarded runs: the first W3 and W8 runs (17:50:05) started in the same millisecond, gave the same job ids and swapped answers. They are kept out of every number here.
  • Reading the site-chat numbers: the site-chat judgings reuse the Perplexity answer from the email run of the same question, so both channels face the same rival text.
  • Files: tests/quality/reports/launch-gate-20261103/ holds the result JSON for every battery, the scorer output and the three helper scripts (lookup-battery, q-stream, mk-rejudge).
