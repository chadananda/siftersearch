## 6. Name-list sources

- **Hands of the Cause**: Wikipedia, [Hands of the Cause](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hands_of_the_Cause), Appointments section. It lists 4 named by Bahá'u'lláh, 4 posthumously by 'Abdu'l-Bahá and 42 by Shoghi Effendi (10 posthumous, 32 living), citing *The Ministry of the Custodians 1957-1963* (Bahá'í World Centre, 1992), pp. xxii-xxiv, and Smith, *A Concise Encyclopedia of the Bahá'í Faith* (2000). Charles Mason Remey is included for completeness; he was later expelled as a Covenant-breaker, and no talks of his were collected.
- **Universal House of Justice members 1963-2023**: Wikipedia, [Universal House of Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_House_of_Justice), past and current members table (31 people), citing Smith (2000) and Bahá'í World News Service election reports. Membership changes after the 2023 election were not checked.
