7Not in launch week

What: what waits until after launch.

Why: two modes done well beat six done roughly. Each item below gets its own plan.

  • Voice, and on-device listening.
  • Tutorials and series.
  • Activities (reading a book together, study plans, discussion).
  • Other channels: messaging apps and text messages.
  • The reader document and long-term memory. Launch week remembers the conversation only.
  • Re-engagement letters after silence (F12), news letters (F13), learning which letters work (F14 bandits).
  • A visible reader profile (F10).
  • Prepared dossiers and topic collections.

9Decisions for Chad

What: the choices only Chad can make, numbered so they can be answered by number.

Why: everything else proceeds without waiting.

  1. Lookups fast path. Today 16 s through the agent, about 5 s before; target ≤1 s. I'll restore the direct path unless you say otherwise.
  2. Launch audience, and the three to five things the launch must show.
  3. Answer models by role: keep the current writing model, or try a cheaper one behind the judge.
  4. Consent for letters (D1): confirm the hybrid: signing in is consent to be remembered and to the welcome sequence, whose first letter says plainly that he will write now and then and how to pause him.
  5. Email allowlist (D7): internal testers only for any email (proposed); chat changes go to everyone. Confirm, and name who is on it.
  6. Letters by the stronger model (D4): confirm. Chat keeps the writing model; a letter may take 30 s and deserves the care.
  7. Open: letter language. Write in the language the person uses, or always English at first.
  8. Open: profile visibility. Whether people will see what he keeps about them, once the reader document exists.
