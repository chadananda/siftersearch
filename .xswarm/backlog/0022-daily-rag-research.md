## The standard
Chad, 2026-09-06:

> The RAG and AI search landscape is constantly evolving. I want to do daily
> research into best practices that might improve our outcome. At present our
> AI results are sub-par to Perplexity, despite having access to copyrighted
> books that Perplexity does not have. The difference is simply Perplexity's
> better search and analysis algorithms. But we can move faster and we have a
> far more limited scope of expertise (interfaith ideas and history) so we
> should be able to outpace the others in this narrow arena. We need the
> attitude that we are never ever satisfied with status quo. Daily ultrathink
> about our search and indexing stack and how it can be faster, smarter and
> more useful to the seeker. Never be satisfied with results that are less
> than amazing.

## The strategic claim to hold onto
The corpus is the moat — copyrighted books Perplexity cannot index. The gap is
algorithmic, and narrow scope is the advantage: a general engine must be
adequate at everything; this one only has to be extraordinary at interfaith
ideas and history. That is a winnable fight and it is the reason to keep pushing.

## Why this is a ritual and not a one-off
Status quo satisfaction is the failure mode named. A single research pass
produces a report; a daily one produces a habit of dissatisfaction.
